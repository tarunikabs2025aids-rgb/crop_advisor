How it works

  1. Farmer raises a ticket with crop and symptom details.
  2. System automatically assigns an active officer from the farmer's region.
  3. Assigned officer gives a recommendation and closes the ticket.
  4. Farmer can reopen a closed ticket if the issue persists.
  5. Tickets pending for more than 48 hours are marked as escalated.

Business Rules

  • Only the assigned officer can close a ticket.
  • Only the ticket-owning farmer can reopen it.
  • Reopening resets the 48-hour resolution timer.
  • A ticket cannot be created when no active officer exists in its region.
